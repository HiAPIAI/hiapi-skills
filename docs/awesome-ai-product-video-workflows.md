# Awesome AI Product Video Workflows

Repository: https://github.com/HiAPIAI/awesome-ai-video-workflows

Skill name: `awesome-ai-product-video-workflows`

Use this source-grounded workflow library to turn product images, ecommerce listings, or product briefs into production plans for hero visuals, product videos, UGC ads, and social variants.

## Workflow Coverage

- Product-image audit
- Product image to commercial hero visual
- Hero visual to product video
- Product page or brief to social video ad
- Creator-style product demo and UGC ad
- TikTok, Reels, Shorts, marketplace variants, and media QC

## Install

```bash
npx -y github:HiAPIAI/awesome-ai-video-workflows -y
```

Use `--codex`, `--claude`, or `--target=/path/to/skills` when a specific destination is required.

Planning and review do not require a HiAPI key. If the selected workflow uses a HiAPI generation skill:
